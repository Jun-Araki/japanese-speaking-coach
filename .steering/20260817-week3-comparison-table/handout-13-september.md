# Japanese Speaking Coach

**A 5-minute practice conversation in Japanese. You get the corrections — and the reason for
each one, in English — after you finish, not while you are talking.**

**nihongo-coach.streamlit.app**

Access code: `____________`  *(I will tell you)*

### How to use it

1. **Open the link on your phone.** Enter the code.
2. **Choose a situation** (greeting, saying thank you, asking for something…) and **Beginner**.
3. **Talk.** Press the microphone and say one sentence in Japanese. Or **type** it.
4. **Keep going for about 5 turns.** Nothing is corrected while you talk — on purpose.
5. **Press "End the conversation"** to see every sentence you said, corrected, with the reason
   in English.

*Stuck on the first line? Try:* 「おはようございます。」 *or* 「はじめまして。〇〇です。」

**On an iPhone, press ▶ to hear the reply.** Safari does not let a page play sound on its own.
On a laptop it plays by itself.

### Two things worth knowing

- **If you want every mistake caught, type instead of speaking.** The app writes down what it
  hears, and it sometimes tidies a mistake up before the correction sees it — so a sentence you
  got wrong can come back as "this one is fine". **Speaking is for practice; typing is for
  corrections.**
- **Nothing you say is stored.** No database, no recording, no log. Closing the page ends the
  session for good. What you say does go to an AI model outside the app to generate the reply,
  so please do not say anything private.

**The voice you hear is AI-generated. It is not a recording of a person.**

### Would you tell me what you thought?

Three lines is plenty, and **"it was confusing" helps me more than "it was good"**.

- Was the reply easy to understand?
- Was the correction useful — did you learn something you did not know?
- What would you change first?
